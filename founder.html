Meet Our Founder

Lynnlieth Patterson, Founder and President, was identified as a gifted learner at age nine. With eleven years of experience in education spanning K–12 and adult English as a Second Language, Christian education, and Montessori practice, Mrs. Patterson brings a broad perspective to individualized learning.

As a homeschooling mother of a gifted child, Mrs. Patterson combines firsthand understanding of family-led education with professional experience in curriculum planning. This work is further informed by graduate studies in Educational Technology and Instructional Design, bringing a thoughtful approach to how learning experiences are structured and personalized.

At the heart of Gifted by Grace is the belief that every child’s gifts deserve to be recognized, nurtured, and given room to grow.
