About Our Founder

Lynnlieth Patterson, Founder and President, was identified as a gifted learner at age nine. Her eleven years of experience in education span K–12 and adult English as a Second Language, Christian education, and Montessori practice.

As a homeschooling mother of a gifted child, she brings firsthand understanding of family-led education to curriculum planning. She is currently pursuing graduate studies in Educational Technology and Instructional Design.

The Homeschool Matchmaker Masterclass

Eight self-paced, faith-centered modules help you discover your child’s gifts, choose curriculum, and build a personalized homeschool plan.

Enrollment opens November 6, 2026. $250 standard or $25 for income-eligible families.

Ask About Enrollment
Find out more about the masterclass

Through guided reflection and practical planning activities, you’ll build a homeschool portfolio shaped around your child’s gifts, your family’s values, and your educational goals. This is parent education—not a prescribed curriculum or a stipend.

Explore classical, Charlotte Mason, Montessori, worldschooling, unschooling, and eclectic approaches without being directed toward one philosophy.

  1. Building Your Homeschool Foundation — Family Vision Statement
  2. Discovering Your Child’s Gifts — Child Gift Profile
  3. Nurturing Your Child’s Gifts — Gift Development Plan
  4. Finding Your Homeschool Match — Homeschool Match Results
  5. Finding Your Curriculum Match — Curriculum Selection Plan
  6. Finding Your Resource Match — Resource and Scholarship Action Plan
  7. Building Your Personalized Learning Plan — Personalized Learning Plan
  8. Bringing It All Together — Complete Homeschool Blueprint

Enrollment includes one year of access to video modules and the curriculum review library. Downloaded resources and completed portfolio documents remain yours.

Annual renewal: $127 standard or $12.70 for income-eligible families. Payment plans are available; contact us for details.
