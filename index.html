Every child has gifts worth nurturing.

Gifted by Grace Learning Haven, Inc. equips Christian families and small learning communities with spiritually grounded guidance, personalized planning, and income-based access to expert support. We serve caregivers of gifted and emerging-gifted learners in grades K–12, with special attention to underserved families and children whose gifts may be obscured by a learning difference.

Our three core services are the Homeschool Matchmaker Masterclass, one-on-one consultations, and curriculum consulting for microschools and small learning communities. All services are delivered online, allowing us to serve families nationwide.

Founded in May 2025, the organization received IRS recognition as a tax-exempt 501(c)(3) in June 2026.

Meet our founder

Lynnlieth Patterson, Founder and President, was identified as a gifted learner at age nine. With eleven years of experience in education spanning K–12 and adult English as a Second Language, Christian education, and Montessori practice, Mrs. Patterson brings a broad perspective to individualized learning.

As a homeschooling mother of a gifted child, Mrs. Patterson combines firsthand understanding of family-led education with professional experience in curriculum planning. This work is further informed by graduate studies in Educational Technology and Instructional Design, bringing a thoughtful approach to how learning experiences are structured and personalized.

At the heart of Gifted by Grace is the belief that every child’s gifts deserve to be recognized, nurtured, and given room to grow.

The Homeschool Matchmaker Masterclass

Learn how to teach the child in front of you.

Eight self-paced, faith-centered modules help caregivers recognize and nurture their child’s gifts, explore homeschool approaches, choose curriculum, find state resources, and build a personalized homeschool portfolio. This is parent education—not a prescribed curriculum or a stipend.

Through guided reflection and practical planning activities, you’ll build a homeschool portfolio shaped around your child’s gifts, your family’s values, and your educational goals.

Families explore classical, Charlotte Mason, Montessori, worldschooling, unschooling, and eclectic approaches without being directed toward one philosophy.

What you’ll build

  1. Building Your Homeschool Foundation — Family Vision Statement
  2. Discovering Your Child’s Gifts — Child Gift Profile
  3. Nurturing Your Child’s Gifts — Gift Development Plan
  4. Finding Your Homeschool Match — Homeschool Match Results
  5. Finding Your Curriculum Match — Curriculum Selection Plan
  6. Finding Your Resource Match — Resource and Scholarship Action Plan
  7. Building Your Personalized Learning Plan — Personalized Learning Plan
  8. Bringing It All Together — Complete Homeschool Blueprint

Pricing and access

Standard enrollment: $250. Grace Tier enrollment: $25 for income-eligible families. Payment plans are available; contact us for details.

Enrollment includes one year of access to video modules and the curriculum review library. Downloaded planning resources and completed portfolio documents remain yours permanently.

Annual renewal: $127 standard or $12.70 for income-eligible families, including updated modules and new reviews. Community access is included at no additional charge and continues after course access expires.
