What We Do

Academic excellence and spiritual growth, together.

Gifted by Grace Learning Haven, Inc. is a nonprofit organization dedicated to providing faith-based, culturally grounded support for families of gifted learners. Our mission is to equip parents and caregivers with the tools, confidence, and community they need to create thriving homeschool environments.

We prioritize serving underserved communities, ensuring that all families — regardless of economic background — have access to quality homeschooling resources and guidance.

We believe every gifted learner deserves personalized support that recognizes their unique needs while fostering strong literacy and STEAM enrichment.
