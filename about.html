Every child has gifts worth nurturing.

Gifted by Grace Learning Haven, Inc. equips Christian families and small learning communities to discover, name, and nurture the gifts God has placed in their children.

Through spiritually grounded guidance, personalized planning, and income-based access to expert support, we help families build an education that fits their child.

Who we serve

  • Caregivers of gifted and emerging-gifted learners in grades K–12.
  • Families beginning or continuing to homeschool.
  • Christian microschools and small learning communities.
  • Underserved families and children whose gifts may be overlooked or obscured by a learning difference.

All services are delivered online, allowing us to serve families nationwide.

Our foundation

Founded in May 2025, Gifted by Grace received IRS recognition as a tax-exempt 501(c)(3) in June 2026.

Our three core services are the Homeschool Matchmaker Masterclass, one-on-one consultations, and curriculum consulting.
