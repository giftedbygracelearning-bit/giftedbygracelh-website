The Homeschool Matchmaker Masterclass

Enrollment opens November 6, 2026.

Eight self-paced, faith-centered modules help you recognize your child’s gifts, explore homeschool approaches, choose curriculum, find state resources, and build a personalized homeschool portfolio.

Through guided reflection and practical planning activities, you’ll build a homeschool portfolio shaped around your child’s gifts, your family’s values, and your educational goals.

This is parent education, not a prescribed curriculum or a stipend. Classical, Charlotte Mason, Montessori, worldschooling, unschooling, and eclectic approaches are explored without directing families toward one philosophy.

Your personalized portfolio

  • A written vision for your homeschool.
  • A profile of your child’s gifts and a plan to nurture them.
  • A curriculum decision suited to your child.
  • A map of resources available in your state.
  • A personalized learning plan.
